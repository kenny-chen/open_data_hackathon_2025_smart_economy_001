## Back-kitchen food safety and security

“Back-kitchen food safety and security” is a low-cost, progressive rodent control solution aimed at assisting the food service industry in avoiding food safety risks caused by rodents.

The system has comprehensive rodent risk management capabilities, enabling accurate identification, measurement, monitoring, and reporting of rodent contamination situations. At the micro level, it employs technologies such as three-dimensional reconstruction, indoor positioning, and image recognition to precisely locate rodent contamination. At the macro level, it achieves prediction of community rodent trends through open data and information from nearby nodes.

In the future, it will continue to focus on food safety issues in the catering industry, helping to establish a good reputation for safe and quality dining.

「后厨食安守卫」是一套低成本丶渐进式的防控鼠害污染解决方案，旨在协助餐饮业规避鼠患引致的食安风险。

系统拥有完整的鼠害风险管理 ，能够准确识别丶计量丶监控和报告鼠害污染情况。在微观层面，采用了三维重建丶室内定位和图像识别等技术，精确定位鼠害污染。在宏观层面，通过开放数据与邻近节点情报，实现预测社区鼠患趋势。

未来，将持续关注餐饮食安问题，帮助建立良好的餐饮安全质量信誉。
